# Romans 1

- Paul wastes no time greeting the Romans, in which he declares the following from Jesus Christ (Rom 1: 3-6):
  - God's Son was promised by the prophets,
  - A descendent of King David,
  - Someone who resurrected from the dead,
  - Named Lord,
  - Through whom we received grace and apostelship.

- The Gospel is the power of God for salvation. To the **Jew first** and then to the Greek (or anyone else, Rom 1: 16).

- The rightousness of God is revealed and live by in faith.

- Some people have known about God's attributes, however, these have been ignored or suppressed by them. They have no excuse (Rom 1: 18-19).

- These people have used idolatry and they have submitted themselves to impurity (Rom 1: 21-24).

- God gives them up to their passions, to their unnatural relations (Rom1: 26-27).

- Paul provides of big list of sins, that those who know God but are unwilling to acknowledge Him, commit (Rom 1: 28-32).
