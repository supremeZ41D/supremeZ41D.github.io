# Romans 1

- Paul wastes no time greeting the Romans, in which he declares Jesus Christ is the Lord, that he rose up from death and that he is a descendent from King David.

- When people believe in God, they will be justified, and blessed.

- For those people who know about God but keep pursuing injustice to others, thus holding back their understanding of God, God will reprimand them. These people may also be considered wise in this world, but they act as fools. They have practiced idolatry (among other sinful acts), to which, as a consequence, God will leave them in those sinful passions
