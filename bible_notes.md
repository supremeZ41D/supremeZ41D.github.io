# Bible Notes

Here you will find a list of pages showcasing some notes I have taken while reading the Bible. As with any interpretation, these pages contain a mix of objective observations and opinion-based insights.

These notes do not represent any single church, denomination, group, or school of thought. Although I have my biases (as all people do, by the way), my intent is never to convince, but rather to highlight what a great book the Bible is.

As I usually think when reflecting on the Bible... this is the **_greatest_** book ever written and a grand inheritance to mankind. Not just the best book... but the greatest book.

Seldom do you see a book transform generations the way this one has. Its central story has literally divided time (we are currently in the year 2026!). It was the first book ever printed. People have died telling its stories and preaching its good news. It has molded cultures and inspired verses in national anthems, music, literature, movies, and paintings.

People have attacked it, as well as those who hold it in the highest regard. Regimes have tried to murder in its name, some others tried banning it, and some schools of thought have even attempted to disregard its influence on modern human history. Yet, people have also followed it, loved it, and held onto it as the highest testament to a higher order of obedience—exposing themselves to both deep respect and harsh persecution. It has been used in courtrooms, hotels, marriage ceremonies, and countless other cultural milestones.

**_No other book has ever held this much influence in our world!_**

It is my personal opinion that if there ever were a book that everyone should read—if you only have space for one single book on your shelf—the Bible should be that book.
